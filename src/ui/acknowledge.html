Pharmacogenomic Cascade Testing

A concept build. Please read this before continuing.

What this is

  • A working demonstration of a fail-closed clinical decision support system, built to show how a confirmed severe cutaneous adverse drug reaction could become a signed familial pharmacogenomic advisory.
  • Its purpose is to show where the system refuses to act. Most of what you will see it do is decline to produce anything, and say exactly why.

What it is not

  • It is not validated, and carries no regulatory approval of any kind. It must not inform any real prescribing decision.
  • The clinical content is a demonstration rule pack. Its citations were checked against primary bibliographic records, which is not the same as a clinician having read and curated them.
  • There is no authentication. The user switcher lets anyone act as any of the seeded demonstration staff. Nothing here establishes who you are.

Do not enter real patient data

  • Everything you type is shared with everyone else using this link, and is discarded when the server restarts. Nothing is written to disk.
  • There is no Data Processing Agreement behind this deployment and no key management. Entering identifiable information about a real person would be inappropriate and is your responsibility, not this system's.
  • Use the seeded demonstration records, or invent details.
