Registry

De-identified and aggregate only. No organisation, no case identifier, no date finer than the month. Any cell below the suppression threshold is withheld, because in a population with an allele frequency of a couple of per cent, a count of one in a named state and month is a person.

Pilot endpoints

The falsifiable prediction is stated in advance: carrier yield in tested relatives should approach 50%. If it returns at population baseline, the thesis is wrong, and the point of measuring is to find that out early and cheaply.
